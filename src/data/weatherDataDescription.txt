This file is for describing the different columns in the weather.csv

STATION:
The unique ID of the weather station that recorded the data

DATE:
The day the weather was recorded

LATITUDE:
The north/south geographic coordinate of the weather station

LONGITUDE:
The east/west geographic coordinate of the weather station

ELEVATION:
The height of the weather station above sea level in meters

NAME:
The name and country of the weather station

TEMP:
The average temperature for the day in Fahrenheit

TEMP_ATTRIBUTES:
The number of observations used to calculate the daily temperature

DEWP:
The average dew point temperature for the day in Fahrenheit

DEWP_ATTRIBUTES:
The number of observations used to calculate the dew point

SLP:
The average sea level pressure for the day in millibars

SLP_ATTRIBUTES:
The number of observations used to calculate sea level pressure

STP:
The average station pressure for the day in millibars

STP_ATTRIBUTES:
The number of observations used to calculate station pressure

VISIB:
The average visibility for the day in miles

VISIB_ATTRIBUTES:
The number of observations used to calculate visibility

WDSP:
The average wind speed for the day in knots

WDSP_ATTRIBUTES:
The number of observations used to calculate wind speed

MXSPD:
The maximum sustained wind speed for the day in knots

GUST:
The maximum wind gust for the day in knots

MAX:
The maximum temperature for the day in Fahrenheit

MAX_ATTRIBUTES:
Indicates whether the max temperature was from an hourly observation or a 6-hour observation

MIN:
The minimum temperature for the day in Fahrenheit

MIN_ATTRIBUTES:
Indicates whether the min temperature was from an hourly observation or a 6-hour observation

PRCP:
The total precipitation for the day in inches

PRCP_ATTRIBUTES:
A flag indicating the time period and quality of the precipitation measurement

SNDP:
The snow depth on the ground in inches

FRSHTT:
A series of flags indicating weather conditions like fog, rain, snow, hail, thunder, and tornado
